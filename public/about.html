About Duna

Duna is a small adaptive learning app for kids: placement over a 1,590-topic skill graph, spaced review, an island map, daily quests and a parent report. It runs on this device, and your child's progress stays on this device.

Where Duna comes from. Duna is developed from Jane, an open-source learning app by Kent Langley and Landon. In the original project the tutor is named Jane, a nod to Jane, the AI companion and teacher to Ender in Orson Scott Card's Ender's Game series (she first appears in Speaker for the Dead). Duna is a separate development of that open-source work: it is not the original project, and it has no affiliation with the author or publishers of the books.
